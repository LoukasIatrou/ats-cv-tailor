Senior Backend Engineer — Platform Reliability
Northwind Cloud (fictional company) — Remote (EU timezones)

About the role

Northwind Cloud's Platform Reliability team owns the services that keep our customers'
payment and billing pipelines running. We're looking for a senior backend engineer who's
comfortable owning a service end to end — design, on-call, and the incident response that
comes with running distributed systems at scale.

What you'll do

- Design and operate distributed backend services in Go, with an emphasis on reliability
  and observability.
- Participate in an on-call rotation and lead incident response for production issues.
- Improve service performance and latency under load (we care a lot about p99, not just
  p50).
- Run infrastructure on Kubernetes and Docker; work closely with our platform/SRE team.
- Mentor other engineers on the team and help set technical direction.
- Collaborate with the payments team on reconciliation and billing correctness.

What we're looking for

- 5+ years of backend engineering experience, with strong Go skills.
- Hands-on experience with Kubernetes, Docker, and cloud infrastructure (AWS preferred).
- Experience owning production services, including on-call and incident response.
- A track record of improving system reliability and performance, not just building new
  features.
- Experience mentoring or leading other engineers.
- Bonus: experience with payments, billing, or other financial infrastructure.
- Bonus: contributions to open-source infrastructure or tooling projects.
